# Hearing & Tinnitus Solutions
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Next.js + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, as requested in Dr. Robert Project.docx. Static export currently published through GitHub Pages, with environment-driven base paths for other hosts.
## Users
Patients and families seeking hearing care and tinnitus support in Pagosa Springs, southwest Colorado, and northwest New Mexico.
## Product Purpose
Explain the practice, help visitors understand care options, meet the providers, and contact the clinic or book an appointment.
## Positioning
Independent audiology practice led by Robert Melton, AuD, CH-TM, with tinnitus-specific training and personalized hearing care.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Dedicated about, hearing, tinnitus, cognition, patient resources, contact, booking, and tinnitus assessment routes. No patient data storage or analytics. The contact popup prepares an email draft in the visitor’s email app, with name, email, and message fields; the website does not submit inquiries to a server. Existing booking integration must be verified; unavailable scheduling must not imply live availability. Draft assessment must not be misrepresented as the validated TFI. Public health claims require sources. User authorized making the GitHub repository public for GitHub Pages hosting on GitHub Free. The user requested a public website link for sharing. Google Maps loads automatically; its behavior is disclosed in the privacy notice.
## Brand Commitments
Clinical, modern, sleek, high whitespace, white navigation, understated teal/navy. Fluid state changes and tactile feedback. Hero and care photographs remain still; the hero uses crossfades only. On iPhone, the user-requested provider story pins the portrait while its background flattens and the bio scrolls over its lower portion. Reduced motion uses a normal stacked layout. Duplicate care shortcuts are hidden on phones, and service tabs stick only within their section. The full provider portrait and name appear before the bio; the name card remains fixed to the portrait as in the preferred iteration; the portrait releases as the final credentials enter view, with no spacer before the next section. All interface text is at least 13px and main reading copy is 16px. No pill navigation. Genuine provider credentials and portraits from current site; generated clinical scenes remain illustrative, with provenance disclosed in the privacy notice; the user requested removal of the hero AI label. User chose building directly from the detailed brief and reviewing the working website.
## Evidence on Hand
../Dr. Robert Project.docx; https://htsaudiology.com/ and its about/services pages. No real staff footage or scheduling-provider access supplied. Do not invent staff identities, patient testimonials, availability, or medical outcomes.
## Product Principles
Clear routes to care. Patient privacy by default. Honest clinical evidence. Accessible motion and readable content.

Motion rhythm: 0.16s presses, 0.7s subtle content reveals, and 1.2s scene transitions. Content remains readable during reveals; images do not drift. Reduced motion preserves final visible states.

On phones, the tinnitus check in uses a native 0 to 10 slider with every level marked; desktop keeps discrete radio ratings. No answer is inferred from the slider’s initial position. Both controls share answer, feedback and score state, with no saved or transmitted responses. The phone provider bio includes one additional paragraph and the existing external screening link.

Bilingual interface: English by default, with a complete Spanish option and a confirmed “También hablamos español” notice. Clinical content and translations require the practice's final review. Social and Google review links appear in the footer and contact page. The displayed Google rating is a dated, owner-supplied snapshot rather than a live feed. The hero slideshow continues during page scrolling and retains manual pause/play. Shared-link previews use Dr. Melton’s original portrait on a light background.
