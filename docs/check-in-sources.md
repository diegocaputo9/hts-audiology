# Tinnitus check-in improvements

The five original 0–10 questions and average-times-ten reflection score remain unchanged. Per-answer feedback describes the selected number, without clinical severity labels. Results chart the five ratings and give educational suggestions for every highest-rated area, including ties. A single high rating is not hidden by the overall average. All-zero answers receive a distinct summary. The questionnaire is not the validated TFI and has no diagnostic cutoffs.

Educational care suggestions: https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/tinnitus
Existing urgent-hearing-loss advice: https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/sudden-deafness

Interaction reference supplied by the user: https://www.affordablehearinglakewood.com/hearing/online-tinnitus-evaluation/ . Its embedded tool uses short questions with educational notes. Our questions, response feedback, rating chart and recommendations are original; we do not reproduce its instrument or statistics.

Answers remain in temporary component memory. There is no server submission, local storage, analytics, account, or email requirement. Browser checks cover answer feedback, chart values, priorities, ties, zero answers, review/reset, mobile overflow, accessibility and absence of form POST requests. Scoring and descriptive feedback have automated tests.
